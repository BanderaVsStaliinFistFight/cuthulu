#

# and cannot be researched. They are granted with set_technology when
# a pact is sealed (rites_1) and each time it is deepened (rites_2/3).
# rites_1 unlocks the god's battalions; every tier makes them stronger.

# Carcosa). eldritch_<god>_incarnation is granted when the god itself is
# incarnated as a division. eldritch_perk_* come from the Prophet's Path.
