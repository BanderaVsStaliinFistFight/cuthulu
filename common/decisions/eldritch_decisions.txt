#####################################################################
# Eldritch Pacts - decisions
#
# 1. Seek the Forbidden Lore         unlocks the summoning rituals
# 2. Summon <god>                    choose ONE patron, ever
# 3. Common rites                    earn Favor, deepen the pact,
#                                    anoint leaders, call servitors
# 4. Patron-specific blessings       timed blessing, avatar, unique rite
# 5. The Prophet                     rites the country leader performs in
#                                    person; most need the leader to bear
#                                    the patron's Herald trait
#
# Favor costs use custom_cost_trigger + custom_cost_text and are paid
# in complete_effect.
#####################################################################
