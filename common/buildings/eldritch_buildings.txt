# Eldritch Pacts - the buildings of the Road to Carcosa
#
# Every country sees them in the construction menu, locked (with a
# tooltip saying so) until a Road to Carcosa chapter unlocks them
# through its technology (eldritch_carcosa_*, see common/technologies),
# so only Hastur's followers can build them. One per state.
#
# Each has its own pictures:
# - icon_frame: its frame in the construction menu's building strip
#   (GFX_buildings_strip, counted from 1). The mod's strip is the
#   game's 31 frames with these four appended as 32 to 35 (drawn by
#   tools/generate_art.py, sprite in interface/eldritch.gfx).
# - special_icon: its icon in the view of the province where one
#   stands (only_display_if_exists). Like the game's own special
#   buildings (facilities, dams, canals, landmarks) they are built in
#   a province (province_max) for that; state_max still allows only
#   one per state.
#####################################################################
